# Sparkline

A trend with no axes, drawn beside the figure it belongs to — usually through `StatTile`'s `sparkline` prop (decision `0069`). It is decorative and `aria-hidden`: the figure beside it carries the number, so **never show one without a figure**. It runs from zero, like every chart here. Fewer than two points, or nothing above zero, draws nothing rather than a flat line that would read as data.

## Accessibility

- `aria-hidden="true"` and `focusable="false"`: it adds nothing a screen reader should hear.
- The figure it sits next to, and the change in words, are what carry the meaning.
