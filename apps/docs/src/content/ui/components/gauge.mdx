# Gauge

One figure against its cap — the month's spend against the budget (decision `0069`). A bar rather than an arc: a length is read faster and more exactly than an angle, and it fits a phone. Both figures are printed above it. Past the cap the bar is full, in the failure colour, and the over-cap state is said in words with `overLabel`. A cap of zero or less draws `emptyLabel`.

## Accessibility

- Both figures are printed; the over-cap state is a sentence with an icon, never colour alone.
- The `<svg>` has `role="img"`, is named by the caption, and each bar's `<title>` holds its figure.
