# Motion

Every movement in the product comes from `ui/styles/motion` and the motion tokens in `ui/styles/variables` — never a duration or an easing typed in a component. Three rules hold for all of it:

1. **Only `transform`, `opacity` and SVG stroke are animated.** Nothing re-lays the page; nothing shifts.
2. **Nothing gates interaction.** An element mid-entrance works; no `pointer-events`, no handler on `animationend`.
3. **Reduced motion is handled once**, by the rule in `ui/styles/base`. The hooks that move things themselves (`useCountUp`, `useInView`) read the preference in script and write final values.

## The in-view trigger

`InView` (and `ui/hooks/useInView` under it) holds every entrance inside it until it is reached. Only an element that is still below the fold when the page wakes up is armed (`data-in-view="waiting"`); reaching it writes `shown` and every entrance inside plays from its start, once. One `IntersectionObserver` serves the whole page. `ChartFrame` and `Rings` use it; the web app's `Reveal` is the same trigger on a landing-page section.

## View Transition names

A card that grows into its page and back shares one `view-transition-name` with the page's hero, named by the thing, never by the screen: `dish-<id>` (a dish card and the dish page's picture), `dish-title-<id>` (its name on the strip and the page's `h1`), `day-<index>` (a plan day's card and that day's page). A name must be unique on the page at the moment of the transition. The routes are wired by the phases that build those screens.
