# LineChart

One or more series over time, drawn as server-rendered SVG from plain arrays (decision `0069`). Days in `YYYY-MM-DD` are placed at their real distance apart, so a missing day is a longer step, not a squeezed one. The scale starts at zero and ends on a round tick. It is not a client component and needs no JavaScript to draw.

Every point carries an SVG `<title>` with its figure. The chart's accessible name is `title`, a legend appears for two series or more, and "show data" holds the same figures as a table. When every value is zero, or there are no labels, it says `emptyLabel` instead of drawing a flat line.

`tone` on a series pins its colour: `1`–`6` for the categorical slots, or `'success' | 'failure' | 'neutral'` for a series that means an outcome. Without it a series takes the slot of its position.

## Accessibility

- The `<svg>` has `role="img"` and is named by the visible caption.
- Every point is a mark with a `<title>` holding its exact figure; past 31 points it is an invisible target instead of a dot.
- "Show data" is a native `<details>` with the figures in a `<table>`, so no number lives only in a shape.
- The axes are `aria-hidden`: the table says what they say.
