# ColumnChart

Columns from a zero baseline, one or more series per label — side by side, or stacked with `stacked` (decision `0069`). The scale always starts at zero and ends on a round tick at or above the tallest column, or the tallest stack. Server-rendered SVG, no JavaScript.

Every column carries a `<title>` with its figure, a legend appears for two series or more, and "show data" holds the figures as a table — with a total column when `stacked` and `totalLabel` are set. All zeros, or no labels, draws `emptyLabel`.

### Stacked outcomes

Series that mean an outcome wear `tone` `'success'`, `'failure'` and `'neutral'`, stacked in that order. A 2px gap in the surface colour separates the segments — the dark pair sits close for colour-blind readers, so the gap is part of the encoding.

## Accessibility

- The `<svg>` has `role="img"` and is named by the visible caption; each column's `<title>` holds its figure.
- A narrow chart keeps the first, middle and last date labels and drops the rest (a container query), so labels never collide.
- The figures are always in the "show data" table.
