# BarChart

Horizontal bars from a zero baseline: the funnel, and any top-N list (decision `0069`). Each row prints its name and its figure as HTML text above a bar whose length is the figure against the longest one, so a long name wraps and the figure drops below it rather than running into it. With `shares`, each figure is followed by a share — the funnel's "of the step before".

One series wears one colour: a bar's length already says how much. Two series or more draw a thinner bar per series in each row, with a legend. All zeros draws `emptyLabel`.

## Accessibility

- Names and figures are printed as text, so nothing depends on reading a length.
- The names and figures are HTML text, read as they are (SVG text cannot wrap, and text inside a `role="img"` is skipped by screen readers); the bars are an `aria-hidden` SVG. The figure is named by the caption. Each bar's `<title>` holds its figure and share for a pointer.
- The figures and shares are in the "show data" table.
