- 2026-09-08 — Nutrition targets are advisory: a plan that drifts from them is delivered with the drift recorded, and only structural faults or safety bounds (minimum energy, protein ceiling) discard it. Recorded as [`0011`](./0011-nutrition-targets-are-advisory.md). (who decided: owner — "los datos son orientativos, nunca tiene que fallar la generación"; agent, the split)
- 2026-09-08 — When the model cannot fill a rotated pool, generation falls back once to the whole safe library — last fortnight's dishes included, no second model call — and records `fallback: full_library` on the plan. Excluding last fortnight ([`0009`](./0009-rotate-reuse-per-user.md)) is a variety preference and, like nutrition targets ([`0011`](./0011-nutrition-targets-are-advisory.md)), a preference never costs someone their plan. (who decided: owner — "nunca tiene que fallar la generación"; agent, the mechanism)
- 2026-09-08 — Ingredient alternatives are pairs between catalogue rows, seeded by hand in families, filtered per person by the same allergen gate as the dish, and shown on every meal — including plans already generated. A swap never introduces a class of food the dish did not have, which keeps a vegetarian's or a halal plan intact without the code knowing the pattern. Staples get none. Recorded as [`0012`](./0012-alternatives-are-catalogue-pairs.md). (who decided: owner, by request — "si una receta lleva lentejas, cámbialo por garbanzos"; agent, the mechanism and the pairs)
- 2026-09-08 — A third of every slot's pool is written fresh by the model whatever the library could fill, so two users' plans differ in content and the library grows with every plan; reuse fills the other two thirds. The catalogue grows from 200 to about a thousand rows, each with a food class, so the substitution rule and, later, dietary patterns can be enforced from data. Recorded as [`0013`](./0013-a-third-of-every-plan-is-fresh.md). (who decided: owner — "no quiero limitar nada, si no todos los planes se parecen muchísimo", and the fraction; agent, the mechanism)
- 2026-09-09 — A person can say once, on the meal screen, whether a dish should come back: liked dishes go to the front of the library pick and are named to the model as their taste; disliked ones are held back from reuse for good and named as never to recreate. One verdict per recipe, on the recipe. Recorded as [`0014`](./0014-a-verdict-shapes-the-next-plan.md). (who decided: owner, by request; agent, the mechanism)
- 2026-09-09 — A fortnight's plan may be redone once (the next fortnight is never rationed; redos are counted along the plan chain) and a plan's meals swapped five times, library first and the model only when the library has nothing for the slot; the shopping list is rebuilt in the same transaction. Two constants in `core/domain/Allowance`, for a premium tier to raise. Recorded as [`0015`](./0015-one-redo-a-fortnight-five-swaps-a-plan.md). (who decided: owner — the two allowances and their numbers; agent, the mechanism)
- 2026-09-09 — Generation asks each slot in its own request, in parallel, and covers a short first round from the library before asking the model again; the generation screen polls with backoff. A meal can be marked eaten or skipped. Recorded as [`0016`](./0016-one-round-per-slot-in-parallel.md). (who decided: owner, by report; agent, the mechanism)
